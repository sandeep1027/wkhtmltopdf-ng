PAGINATION-PAGE-01

This document is six pages long. Separate header and footer HTML files stamp [page] / [topage] on every sheet.

What to look for

  1. Header band: title on the left, running label, current page on the right.
  2. Footer band: first page number, a different label, and page/total.
  3. Numbers advance: page 1 of 6 through page 6 of 6.
